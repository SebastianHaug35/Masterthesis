The text below is about the account payable process. It begins when you send to the receiving department. When sending to the receiving department is completed, you need to create a receiving report. After creating a receiving report, the next tasks should be gathering the vendors invoice and doubling check a three-way match, they should be done in parallel. After doubling check a three-way match, confirming all documents have been amended needs to be done. Once all pre-tasks are executed, then stamping all documents should be done. The process is now completed.
