The text below is about the account payable process. It begins when you send to the receiving department. Once sending to the receiving department occurs, you need to create a receiving report. When creating a receiving report is completed, the process is split into 3 parallel branches: doubling check a three-way match, gathering the vendors invoice and confirming all documents have been amended. After gathering the vendors invoice, gathering the receiving report should be done. Once confirming all documents have been amended occurs, reporting errors to relevant team member should be done. Once all pre-tasks are executed, then you need to schedule the payment. The process is now completed.
