The following text is about the account payable process. It begins when you send to the senior accountant in your department. Once sending to the senior accountant in your department occurs, creating a receiving report needs to be done. After creating a receiving report, you need to gather the vendors invoice. When gathering the vendors invoice is completed, the next tasks should be entering the invoice into the accounts payable account and stamping all documents, they should be done in parallel. When entering the invoice into the accounts payable account is completed, you need to store in paid invoice file. The process is now completed.
