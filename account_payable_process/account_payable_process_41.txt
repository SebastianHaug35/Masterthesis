The following description is about the account payable process. It starts with sending to the senior accountant in your department. After sending to the senior accountant in your department, creating a receiving report should be done. When creating a receiving report is completed, one or more of the following paths should be executed: doubling check a three-way match, reporting errors to relevant team member. After that, the process is split into 2 parallel branches: creating voucher for the documents and storing in paid invoice file. After storing in paid invoice file, scheduling the payment needs to be done. The process is now completed.
