The text below is about the account payable process. It starts with entering purchase order details. Once entering purchase order details occurs, creating a receiving report needs to be done. After creating a receiving report, gathering the receiving report should be done. When gathering the receiving report is completed, the process is split into 2 parallel branches: checking dates to avoid penalty charges and entering the invoice into the accounts payable account. Once checking dates to avoid penalty charges occurs, creating voucher for the documents needs to be done. The process is now completed.
