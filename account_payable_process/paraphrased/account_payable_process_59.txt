The following text is about the account payable process. It starts with a return to the vendor accounting department. When the transmission to the vendor accounting department is completed, a receiving report should be created. After creating a receiving report, the vendors invoice should be collected. After the gathering of the vendors' invoices, the next tasks should be arranging the payment and storing in the paid invoice file, and they should be done in parallel. After the payment is scheduled, stamping should be done on all documents. Once storing in the paid invoice file occurs, the next tasks should be entering the invoice into the accounts payable and creating the voucher for documents, this should be done in parallel. The process is now complete.
