The text below is about the invoice process, and starts with a copy for internal records. When filing a copy for internal records is completed, the creation of a receiving report should be done. After creating a receiving report you will collect the vendors' invoice. After gathering the invoice of vendors, the process is divided into 3 parallel branches : entering invoice into the account payables account, creating a coupon for the documents and analyzing dates to avoid penalty charges. The process is now complete.
