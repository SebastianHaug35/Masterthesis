The following description is about the Account payable process, which starts when you transmit the letter to the receiving department. After sending to the Receiving Department, you have to create a Receiving report. Once a receiving report is created, collecting the vendor invoice must be done. Once the vendor invoice gathering occurs, the next steps should be storing in the invoice file and scheduling payments, they should be done in parallel. Once the payment is scheduled, the next tasks for creating vouchers for the documents and calculating dates should be to avoid penalty charges. They should be done in parallel. The process is now done.
