The following description is about the account payable process and starts with sending to the senior accountant in your department. When the report is completed to the senior accountant in your department, you need to create a receipt When creating a receiving report is completed, a three-way match double check is required. Once a triple check appears, the next task should be the scheduling of the payment and creating a voucher for the documents, they should be done in parallel. The process is now finished.
