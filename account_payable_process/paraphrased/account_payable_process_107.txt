The following text is about the payable process that begins when you send a letter to the senior accountant in your department. Once the send to the senior accountant in your department happens, creating a receiving report needs to be completed. Once creating a receiving report occurs, the process is split into 2 parallel branches: verifying all documents have been amended and gathering the vendor invoices. Once the vendor invoice collection occurs, a three-way match must be doubled. Once all of the pre-tasks are executed you must check the date to avoid penalties. The process is now complete.
