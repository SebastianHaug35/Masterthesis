The following description is about the process of Account payable, which starts with a copy of a workbook for internal records. After filing a copy for internal documents, a receiving report should be created. After a receiving report is created, the next tasks should be to collect the receiving report and doubling check a three-way match, they should be done in parallel. Once all pre-tasks are completed then the storing of the paid invoice file needs to be done. The process is now completed.
