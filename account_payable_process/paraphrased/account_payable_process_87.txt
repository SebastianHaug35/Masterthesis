The following is about the accounting payable process which begins when you send the senior accountant in your department. Once a sending occurs to the senior accountant in your department, you need to create a receipt report. When the creation of a receiving report is completed the next tasks should be to doubling check a three-way match and gather the receiving report, they should be done in parallel. After doubling a three-way match, the process is split into 2 parallel branches: reporting errors to the relevant team member and collecting the vendor invoice. After submitting the invoice to vendors, you must confirm all documents have been amended. Once all pre-tasks are executed, you must store the payment in the invoice file. The process is now completed.
