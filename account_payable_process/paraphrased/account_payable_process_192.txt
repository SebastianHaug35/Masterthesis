The following text is about the account payable process, which starts when you submit to the senior accountant in your department. Once the sending of the Senior Accountant in your department happens, a receiving report needs to be created. After creating a receiving report, you need to collect the vendor invoice. When gathering the vendors'invoice is completed, the next task should be entering the invoice into the accounts payable's account and stamping all documents. It should be done in parallel. When entering the invoice into accounts payable account is completed, you need to store it in the paid invoice file. The process is now completed.
