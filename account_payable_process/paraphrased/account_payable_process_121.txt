The following text is about the invoice process - it starts when you enter Purchase Order details. After entering the purchase order details, a receiving report needs to be done. After creating a receipt report, you need to confirm that all documents have been amended. When the confirmation of all documents have been amended is completed, the process is split into 2 parallel branches : the entering the invoice into the accounts payable account and the storing in the paid invoice file. When the invoice is entered into the account payable, all documents need to be stamped. The process is now complete.
