The following description is about the account payable process, which begins when you send to the receiving department. After sending to the receiving department, a receiving report should be created. After creating a receiving report, a three-way match must be checked. When a three-way match is completed during the double check the process is split into 3 parallel branches : creating a voucher for the documents, storing in the paid invoice file and entering the invoice into the accounts payable account. The process is now completed.
