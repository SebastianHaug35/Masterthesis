The text below is about the account payable process which begins when you enter the details of the purchase order. After entering the purchase order details, you need to create a receiving report. Once a receiving report occurs, one or more of the following paths should be executed: reporting errors to the relevant team member, collecting the receiving report, collecting the invoices of vendors. Once the vendor bill gathering occurs, one or more of the following paths should be executed: Double checking a three-way match, confirming all documents have been amended. Then the process is split into 3 parallel branches : stamping all documents, storing in the paid invoice file and checking dates to avoid penalty charges. After stamping all the documents, you need to create a voucher for the documents. After storing in the paid invoice file, one or more of the following paths should be executed : entering the invoice into the accounts payable account and scheduling the payment. Now the process has been completed.
