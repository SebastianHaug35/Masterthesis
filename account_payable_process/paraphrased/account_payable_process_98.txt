The text below is about the account payable process which begins when you send the senior accountant in your department. After sending to the senior accountant in your department, a receiving report should be done. When a receiving report is completed, the vendor invoice collection should be done. Once the collection of vendor invoice occurs, one or more of the following paths should be executed: recording the payment, storing it in the paid invoice file, creating the voucher for documents The process has now been completed.
