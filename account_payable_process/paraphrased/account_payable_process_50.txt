The text below is about the account payable process, which begins when you send to the receiving department. Once a receipt is sent to the receiving department, a receipt report needs to be created. When a Receiver report is completed, you need to double check a 3-way match. Once doubling checks a three-way match occurs, one or more of the following path should be executed : storing invoice file in the payers account, entering invoice into the accounts payable account, checking the dates to avoid penalty charges. Once the invoice is entered into the accounts payable account, payment must be arranged. The process is now completed.
