The following is the account payable process and begins when you send your financial support to the senior accountant in your department. After sending to the senior accountant in your department, create a receipt report. After creating a receipt report, the next task should be the gathering of the receiving report and verification that all documents have been amended. This should be done in parallel. After verifying all documents have been amended, you need to double-check a three-way match. Once all pre-tasks are completed, then you need to check the dates to avoid penalty charges. The process is now complete.
