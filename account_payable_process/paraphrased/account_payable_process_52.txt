The following description is about the payee process. It begins when you enter purchase order details. Once purchasing order details are entered, the creation of a receipt report is done. When a receiving report is created, one or more of the following paths should be executed : reporting errors to the relevant team member, double checking a three-way match. After that, one or more of the following paths should be executed: storing in the paid invoice file, checking the dates to avoid penalties. The process is now complete.
