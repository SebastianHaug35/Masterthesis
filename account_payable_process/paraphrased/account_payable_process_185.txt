The text below is relating to the account payable process that starts with the registration of the Purchase Order details. Once the purchase order details enter and you have to create a receipt report, the report should be created. When the creation of a Receiving Report is completed, one or more of the following paths should be executed : reporting errors to the relevant team member, collecting the vendor invoice, confirming all documents have been amended. After this, dates should be checked to avoid penalty charges. The process is now completed.
