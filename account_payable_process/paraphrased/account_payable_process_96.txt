The following description is about the payable process and starts with the account submitted by the senior accountant in your department. Once the sending occurs to the senior accountant in your department, you need to create a receipt report. After generating a receiving report, one or more of the following paths should be executed : double checking a three-way match, reporting errors to relevant team member, and confirming that all documents have been amended. Then the process is divided into 3 parallel branches : ordering the payment, stamping all documents and storing in the paid invoice file. After stamping all documents, you need to enter the invoice into the payable account. The process is now completed.
