The following text is about the payable account process. It starts with the payment to the receiving department. When the sending to the receiving department is completed, you need to create a receiving report. When the creation of a receiving report is completed, the process is split into 2 parallel branches : gathering the receiving report and reporting errors to the relevant team member. Once all pre-trials are executed then it must be done creating vouchers for the documents. The process is now completed.
