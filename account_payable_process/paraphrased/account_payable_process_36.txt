The following text is about the account payable process which begins when you send the senior accountant in your department. When the communication is completed with your senior accountant in your department, you need to create a receiving report. Once creating a receiving report occurs, one or more of the following paths should be executed: collecting the receiving report and doubling check a three-way match. Once a three-way match occurs through the double check, one or more of the following paths should be executed: confirming the vendors invoice and reporting errors to the relevant team member. The next task should be to stamp all documents, charge the payment and enter the invoice in the account payable. They should be performed in parallel. The process is now complete.
