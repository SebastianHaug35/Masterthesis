The following text is about the account payable process that starts with the sending to the senior accountant in your department. After sending to the senior accountant in your department, a receiving report is to be created. When creating a Receiving Report is completed, one or more of the following paths should be executed : Receiving the invoice of the vendor, Gathering the Receiving Report. After the Collection of the Vendors invoice, you must confirm that all documents have been changed. Also, if necessary, all documents must be stamped. The process is now done.
