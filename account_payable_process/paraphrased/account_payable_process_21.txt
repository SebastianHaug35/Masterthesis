The following is the account payable process: It begins when you email a originating department. When the sending to the receiving department is completed, a receiving report should be created. Once a receiving report comes in, you should confirm all documents have been amended. After confirming all documents have been amended the process is split into 2 parallel branches: creating the document voucher and putting the invoice into the account payable account. Upon the creation of a document voucher, checking dates to avoid penalty charges is needed. When the entry of the invoice into the Accounts payable account is completed, one or more of the following paths should be executed: storing the invoice in a paid account, stamping all documents, scheduling the payment. The process is now completed.
