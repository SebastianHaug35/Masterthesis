The following description is about the account payable process, which begins when you send invoice to the vendor accounting department. Once the delivery to the vendor accounting department occurs, a receipt should be created. When the creation of a receiving report is completed, the next tasks should be the gathering of receiving report and double check a three-way match, they should be done in parallel. Once all pre-tasks have been executed the next tasks should be scheduling the payment and submitting the invoice to the accounts payable, these should be done in parallel. Once the payment has occurred, one or more of the following paths should be executed: stamping all documents, storing in the payable invoice file. The process is now completed.
