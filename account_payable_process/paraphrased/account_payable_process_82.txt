The following text is about the account payable process which begins when you enter the details of your purchase order. When purchase order details are completed, a receipt should be created. Once the creation of a receiving report occurs, you need to verify that all documents have been amended. When all documentation has been changed, the next task should be the balances, the stamping of all documents and entering the invoice to the accounts payable account - they should be done in parallel. Once the payment occurs you have to check the dates to avoid penalty charges. When stamping all documents is completed, creating the voucher for the documents should be done. The process is now complete.
