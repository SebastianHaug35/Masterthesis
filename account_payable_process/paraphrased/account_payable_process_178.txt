The following description is about the account payable process and begins when you send the vendor accounting department. Once the sending occurs to the vendor accounting department, a receiving report should be a done. After creating a receiving report, errors must be reported to the relevant team member. After reporting the errors to the relevant team member, one or more of the following paths should be executed: checking the dates to avoid penalty charges, entering the invoice into the accounts payable account. The process is completed now.
