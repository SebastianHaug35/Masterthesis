The below text is about the account payable process which begins when you send an order to the receiving department. After sending the notification to the Receiving department, you must create a Receiving report. After creating a Receiver Report, you need to double check a three-way match. After doubling a three-way match, the process is split into 2 parallel branches: billing and creating vouchers for the documents. When the payment is completed, one or more of the following paths should be executed: stamping all documents, storing in the paid invoice file. Once stamping all documents occurs, you should enter the invoice into the accounts payable account. After storing in paid invoice file, a check to avoid penalty charges must be done. The process is now completed.
