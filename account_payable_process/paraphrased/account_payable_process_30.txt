The text below is about the account payable process. It begins when you file a copy of the internal records. After filing a copy of internal records, a receiving report must be done. When creating a receiving report occurs the next task should be doubling a three-way match and confirming all documents have been amended, they should be performed in parallel. Once all pre-tasks have been executed, then you need to take the invoice into the accounts payable. The process is now complete.
