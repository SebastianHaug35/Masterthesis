The below text is about the account payable process, which starts with the entry of the Purchase Order Details. When the purchase order details are entered, a receipt report should be created. When creating a receipt report is completed, the next tasks should be to gather the vendors'invoice and confirm all documents have been updated. They should be done in parallel. After confirming that all documents have been updated, the next tasks should be collecting the receiving report, doubling the check of a three-way match and reporting errors to the relevant team member. Once all pre-tasks have been performed then the next tasks should create vouchers for documents and check dates to avoid penalty charges ; they should be done parallel to that. The process is now complete.
