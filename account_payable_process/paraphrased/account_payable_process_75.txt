The text below is about the contract billing process that begins with sending to the receiving department. When sending to the receiving department is completed, a receipt report should be generated. When a receiving report is created the process is split into 2 parallel branches : gathering the receiving report and confirming that all documents have been modified. When collecting the received report is completed the next tasks should be collecting the vendors invoice and doubling the checks a three-way match, they should be done in parallel. When the acquisition of vendors invoice is completed you need to report errors to the relevant team member. Once all the pre-tasks are executed, then you can create a voucher for the documents. The process is now completed.
