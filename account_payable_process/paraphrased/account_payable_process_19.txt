The text below is about the Account payable Process. It begins with the sending to the vendor accounting department. Once the sending occurs to the vendor accounting department, you will need to create a receipt report. Upon creation of a receiving report the next tasks should be reporting errors to the relevant team member, gathering the receiving report and doubling a three-way match, these should be done in parallel. When reporting errors to the relevant team member is completed, confirming that all documents have been amended should be done. Once all pre-tasks have been executed, then you must check the dates to avoid penalty charges. The process is now completed.
