The below text is about the credit payable process and begins when you send to the Receiving department. When the send to the receiving department is completed, you need to generate a receiving report. After creating a receiving report the next tasks should be gathered the invoice of the vendors and doubling check a three-way match ; they should be completed in parallel. After double check a three-way match, confirm all documents have been amended must be done. Once all pre-tasks are completed, stamping should be done for all documents. The process is now complete.
