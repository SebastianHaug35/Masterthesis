The text below is about the account payable process that begins when you send your cheque to the receiving department. After sending your package to the receiving department, you must create a receipt report. When the creation of a receiving report is completed, the next tasks should be confirming all documents are amended and reporting errors to the relevant team member should be done in parallel. Once all pre-tasks are completed, then the bill needs to be entered into accounts payable account. The process is now complete.
