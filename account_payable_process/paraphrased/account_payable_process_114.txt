The following description is about the accounts payable process, which begins when you send mail to the receiving department. When the sending to the receiving department is completed, you need to create a receiving report. After generating a receiving report, gathering of the vendor's invoice is to be done. Once the billation of the vendors takes place, the process is split into 2 parallel branches : checking the dates to avoid penalty charges and entering the invoice into the accounts payable account. The process is now complete.
