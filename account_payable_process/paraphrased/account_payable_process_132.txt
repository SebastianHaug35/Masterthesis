The following description is about the account payable process that starts when you send the receiving department. Once a sender occurs to the receiving department, it needs to produce a receiving report. After creating a receiving report, you need to report errors to the relevant member of the team. After reporting errors to the relevant team member, one or more of the following paths should be executed : stamping all documents, inserting the invoice into the Accounts payable account. When the stamping is complete, the storage of invoices must be done in paid file. The process is now completed.
