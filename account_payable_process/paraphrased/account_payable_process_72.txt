The following description is about the Account payable process which starts with the send to the senior accountant in your department. Once the send to senior accountant in your department occurs, the creating of a receiving report has to be done. Once a receipt report is created the next tasks should be reporting errors to the relevant team member, collecting vendor invoice, and double check a three way match, they should be done in parallel. When the check is completed a three-way match must be done collecting the receiving report. Once all pre-tasks are completed, the next task should be storing the paid invoice file, checking dates to avoid penalties and stamping all documents in parallel. The process is now complete.
