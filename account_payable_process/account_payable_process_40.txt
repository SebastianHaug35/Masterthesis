The text below is about the account payable process. It starts with sending to the receiving department. Once sending to the receiving department occurs, creating a receiving report needs to be done. When creating a receiving report is completed, the process is split into 2 parallel branches: doubling check a three-way match and reporting errors to relevant team member. Once all pre-tasks are executed, then one or more of the following paths should be executed: creating voucher for the documents, entering the invoice into the accounts payable account. The process is now completed.
