The following description is about the account payable process. It begins when you send to the vendor accounting department. When sending to the vendor accounting department is completed, creating a receiving report needs to be done. After creating a receiving report, the process is split into 2 parallel branches: reporting errors to relevant team member and doubling check a three-way match. Once reporting errors to relevant team member occurs, you need to confirm all documents have been amended. Once all pre-tasks are executed, then you need to check dates to avoid penalty charges. The process is now completed.
