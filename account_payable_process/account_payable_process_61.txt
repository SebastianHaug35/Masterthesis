The text below is about the account payable process. It begins when you send to the receiving department. After sending to the receiving department, you need to create a receiving report. After creating a receiving report, you need to double check a three-way match. After doubling check a three-way match, the process is split into 2 parallel branches: scheduling the payment and creating voucher for the documents. When scheduling the payment is completed, one or more of the following paths should be executed: stamping all documents, storing in paid invoice file. Once stamping all documents occurs, you need to enter the invoice into the accounts payable account. After storing in paid invoice file, checking dates to avoid penalty charges needs to be done. The process is now completed.
