The process starts with a manual trigger. After that, a receiving report is created. Then a decision is made and one of the following paths is taken: gathering the vendor invoice or double checking the three-way match. After the validation paths are merged, the process continues with three parallel activities: scheduling the payment, storing the paid invoice file, and creating the voucher for the documents. Once the payment scheduling path continues, the invoice is entered into accounts payable. After the payment-related paths are completed, the process ends.
