The following text is about the account payable process. It begins when you send to the senior accountant in your department. Once sending to the senior accountant in your department occurs, you need to create a receiving report. When creating a receiving report is completed, reporting errors to relevant team member needs to be done. After reporting errors to relevant team member, the process is split into 2 parallel branches: stamping all documents and entering the invoice into the accounts payable account. The process is now completed.
