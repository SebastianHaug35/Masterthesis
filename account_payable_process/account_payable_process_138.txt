The text below is about the account payable process. It starts with filing a copy for internal records. When filing a copy for internal records is completed, creating a receiving report should be done. After creating a receiving report, you need to gather the vendors invoice. After gathering the vendors invoice, the process is split into 3 parallel branches: entering the invoice into the accounts payable account, creating voucher for the documents and checking dates to avoid penalty charges. The process is now completed.
