The following description is about the account payable process. It begins when you send to the receiving department. After sending to the receiving department, creating a receiving report should be done. After creating a receiving report, the process is split into 2 parallel branches: reporting errors to relevant team member and confirming all documents have been amended. When confirming all documents have been amended is completed, you need to gather the receiving report. Once all pre-tasks are executed, then checking dates to avoid penalty charges needs to be done. The process is now completed.
