The following text is about the account payable process. It starts with sending to the vendor accounting department. When sending to the vendor accounting department is completed, creating a receiving report should be done. After creating a receiving report, gathering the vendors invoice should be done. After gathering the vendors invoice, the next tasks should be scheduling the payment and storing in paid invoice file, they should be done in parallel. After scheduling the payment, stamping all documents should be done. Once storing in paid invoice file occurs, the next tasks should be entering the invoice into the accounts payable account and creating voucher for the documents, they should be done in parallel. The process is now completed.
