The text below is about the account payable process. It begins when you enter purchase order details. Once entering purchase order details occurs, you need to create a receiving report. After creating a receiving report, gathering the vendors invoice needs to be done. Once gathering the vendors invoice occurs, the process is split into 3 parallel branches: scheduling the payment, stamping all documents and entering the invoice into the accounts payable account. After scheduling the payment, the next tasks should be checking dates to avoid penalty charges and storing in paid invoice file, they should be done in parallel. Once checking dates to avoid penalty charges occurs, creating voucher for the documents should be done. The process is now completed.
