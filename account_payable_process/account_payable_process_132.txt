The following description is about the account payable process. It begins when you send to the receiving department. Once sending to the receiving department occurs, creating a receiving report needs to be done. After creating a receiving report, you need to report errors to relevant team member. After reporting errors to relevant team member, one or more of the following paths should be executed: stamping all documents, entering the invoice into the accounts payable account. When stamping all documents is completed, storing in paid invoice file needs to be done. The process is now completed.
