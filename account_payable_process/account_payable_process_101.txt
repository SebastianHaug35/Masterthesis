The text below is about the account payable process. It begins when you send to the vendor accounting department. Once sending to the vendor accounting department occurs, creating a receiving report should be done. Once creating a receiving report occurs, one or more of the following paths should be executed: confirming all documents have been amended, reporting errors to relevant team member. After confirming all documents have been amended, doubling check a three-way match should be done. Afterwards, checking dates to avoid penalty charges needs to be done. The process is now completed.
