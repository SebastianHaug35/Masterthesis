The text below is about the account payable process. It begins when you send to the senior accountant in your department. Once sending to the senior accountant in your department occurs, creating a receiving report needs to be done. After creating a receiving report, one or more of the following paths should be executed: doubling check a three-way match, confirming all documents have been amended, reporting errors to relevant team member. Afterwards, creating voucher for the documents needs to be done. The process is now completed.
