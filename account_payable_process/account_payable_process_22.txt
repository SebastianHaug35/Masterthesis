The following text is about the account payable process. It begins when you send to the senior accountant in your department. Once sending to the senior accountant in your department occurs, creating a receiving report needs to be done. Once creating a receiving report occurs, one or more of the following paths should be executed: doubling check a three-way match, gathering the receiving report, reporting errors to relevant team member. Once gathering the receiving report occurs, you need to confirm all documents have been amended. When reporting errors to relevant team member is completed, gathering the vendors invoice should be done. Then, scheduling the payment needs to be done. The process is now completed.
