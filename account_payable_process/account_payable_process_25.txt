The text below is about the account payable process. It starts with sending to the vendor accounting department. After sending to the vendor accounting department, creating a receiving report should be done. When creating a receiving report is completed, one or more of the following paths should be executed: reporting errors to relevant team member, gathering the vendors invoice. When reporting errors to relevant team member is completed, the next tasks should be confirming all documents have been amended, doubling check a three-way match and gathering the receiving report, they should be done in parallel. After that, checking dates to avoid penalty charges should be done. The process is now completed.
