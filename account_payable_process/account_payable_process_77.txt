The following text is about the account payable process. It begins when you send to the senior accountant in your department. When sending to the senior accountant in your department is completed, creating a receiving report needs to be done. When creating a receiving report is completed, the next tasks should be reporting errors to relevant team member and gathering the vendors invoice, they should be done in parallel. Once all pre-tasks are executed, then one or more of the following paths should be executed: storing in paid invoice file, checking dates to avoid penalty charges. The process is now completed.
