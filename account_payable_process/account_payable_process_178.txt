The following description is about the account payable process. It begins when you send to the vendor accounting department. Once sending to the vendor accounting department occurs, creating a receiving report should be done. After creating a receiving report, reporting errors to relevant team member needs to be done. After reporting errors to relevant team member, one or more of the following paths should be executed: checking dates to avoid penalty charges, entering the invoice into the accounts payable account. The process is now completed.
