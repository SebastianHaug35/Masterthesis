The text below is about the account payable process. It starts with entering purchase order details. Once entering purchase order details occurs, creating a receiving report should be done. When creating a receiving report is completed, you need to confirm all documents have been amended. When confirming all documents have been amended is completed, one or more of the following paths should be executed: entering the invoice into the accounts payable account, stamping all documents, scheduling the payment. The process is now completed.
