The following text is about the account payable process. It begins when you enter purchase order details. When entering purchase order details is completed, creating a receiving report should be done. Once creating a receiving report occurs, you need to confirm all documents have been amended. When confirming all documents have been amended is completed, the next tasks should be scheduling the payment, stamping all documents and entering the invoice into the accounts payable account, they should be done in parallel. Once scheduling the payment occurs, you need to check dates to avoid penalty charges. When stamping all documents is completed, creating voucher for the documents should be done. The process is now completed.
