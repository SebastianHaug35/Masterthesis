# account payable process 125  ## Source Description  The following description is about the Account payable process. It starts with sending to the Senior Accountant in your department. You need to create a receipt report after sending it to your senior accountant in your department. Reporting errors to the relevant team member is necessary after the creation of a receiving report. After reporting errors to the relevant team member, the process is split into 2 parallel branches : checking dates to avoid penalty charges and stamping all documents. The process is now completed.  ## Artifacts  - `account_payable_process_125.gv` - `account_payable_process_125.pdf`
