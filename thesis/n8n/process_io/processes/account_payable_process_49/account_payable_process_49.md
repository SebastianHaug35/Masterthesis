# account payable process 49  ## Source Description  The following text is on the account payable process and starts with the sending department. When the sending process to the receiving department is completed, a receiving report needs to be created. After creating a receiving report the process is split into two parallel branches : doubling a three-way match & reporting errors to relevant team member. Once reporting errors to the relevant team member occurs, you need to gather the vendor invoice. Once all pre-tasks are executed, one or more of the following steps should be done: entering the invoice into the accounts payable account, stamping all documents, checking dates to avoid penalty charges. The process is now completed.  ## Artifacts  - `account_payable_process_49.gv` - `account_payable_process_49.pdf`
