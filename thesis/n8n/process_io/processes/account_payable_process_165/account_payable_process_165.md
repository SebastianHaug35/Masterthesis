# account payable process 165  ## Source Description  The following text is about the account payable process. It begins when you send to the senior accountant in your department. Once a copy is sent to the senior accountant in your department, you have to create a receiving report. When creating a receiving report is completed, errors need to be reported to the relevant team member. After reporting errors to the relevant team member, the process is split into 2 parallel branches: stamping all documents and entering the invoice into the payable account. Now the process is complete.  ## Artifacts  - `account_payable_process_165.gv` - `account_payable_process_165.pdf`
