# account payable process 171  ## Source Description  The following text is about the payable process which starts when you send to the receiving department. When sending to the receiving department is completed, a receiving report needs to be created. After creating a receiving report, the process is split into 2 parallel branches : confirming that all documents have been amended and gathering the receiving report. Once the gathering of receiving report occurs, the process is split into 2 parallel branches : reporting the errors to the relevant team member and doubling check a three-way match. When the reporting of errors to the relevant team member is completed, the vendors invoice needs to be done. When all pre-tasks are executed, then one or more of the following paths should be executed: filing the invoice to pay and scheduling the payment. The process is completed now.  ## Artifacts  - `account_payable_process_171.gv` - `account_payable_process_171.pdf`
