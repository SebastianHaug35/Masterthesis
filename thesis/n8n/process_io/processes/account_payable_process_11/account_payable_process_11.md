# account payable process 11  ## Source Description  The text below is about the account payable process that starts with a send to the vendor accounting department. After sending to the vendor accounting department, a receiving report should be created. After creating a receiving report, the report to the relevant team member should be done. When reporting of errors is completed to the relevant team member, the process is split into 2 parallel branches : storing in paid invoice file and scheduling the payment. The process is now completed.  ## Artifacts  - `account_payable_process_11.gv` - `account_payable_process_11.pdf`
