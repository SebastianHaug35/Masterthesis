# account payable process 8  ## Source Description  The following text is about the Account payable process. It starts when you send the vendor accounting department. Once the sending to the vendor accounting department takes place, a receiving report should be created. When you complete the creating a receiving report, you need to collect the vendor invoice. Once the payment of vendors is collected, one or more of the following paths should be executed: The invoice is entered into the account payable and a payment schedule is scheduled. The process is now concluded.  ## Artifacts  - `account_payable_process_8.gv` - `account_payable_process_8.pdf`
