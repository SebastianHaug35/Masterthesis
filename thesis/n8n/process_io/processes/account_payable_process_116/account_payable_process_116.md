# account payable process 116  ## Source Description  The following text is about the account payable process and starts with a payment to the senior accountant in your department. When sending to the senior accountant in your department is completed, a receiving report should be created. When a receiving report is completed, gathering the receiving report is required. After gathering the receiving report, you must enter the invoice to the account payable account. The process is now complete.  ## Artifacts  - `account_payable_process_116.gv` - `account_payable_process_116.pdf`
