# account payable process 25  ## Source Description  The below text is about the process of the account payable and begins with e-mail to the vendor's accounting department. After sending the vendor - accounting department, a receiving report should be created. When a receipt is completed, one or more of the following steps should be performed: reporting errors to the relevant team member, obtaining vendor invoices. When the reporting to the relevant team member is completed, the next tasks should be confirming that all documents have been amended, double checking a three-way match and gathering the receiving report. Immediately after that, be prepared to check dates to avoid penalties. The process is now complete.  ## Artifacts  - `account_payable_process_25.gv` - `account_payable_process_25.pdf`
