# account payable process 144  ## Source Description  The following text is about the credit process. It starts with e-mailing the senior accountant in your department. Once a sending occurs to the senior accountant in your department, a receiving report must be created. Once creating a receiving report occurs, collecting the invoice for vendors should be done. After collecting the invoice of the vendor, you need to check the dates to avoid fines and penalties. The process is now complete.  ## Artifacts  - `account_payable_process_144.gv` - `account_payable_process_144.pdf`
