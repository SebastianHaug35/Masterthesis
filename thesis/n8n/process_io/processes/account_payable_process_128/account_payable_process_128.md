# account payable process 128  ## Source Description  The text below is about the creditor process which begins when you send to the receiving department. After receiving to the receiving department, creating a reception report needs to be done. When a receiving report is created, the process is divided into 2 parallel branches: acquiring the vendor invoice, and assembling the receiving report. When gathering the vendor invoice is completed, the process is split into 2 parallel branches: reporting errors to the relevant team member and confirming all documents have been modified. After reporting errors to the appropriate team member, a three-way match needs to be checked double. Once all pre-tasks are executed, the process is split into 2 parallel branches : entering invoices into accounts payable account and storing in paid invoice file. The process is now complete.  ## Artifacts  - `account_payable_process_128.gv` - `account_payable_process_128.pdf`
