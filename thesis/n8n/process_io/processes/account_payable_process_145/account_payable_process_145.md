# account payable process 145  ## Source Description  The following text is about the payment of your account. It starts with sending to the senior accountant in your department. Once a report reaches the Senior Accountant in your department, it should be completed creating a receipt report. When the creation of a receiving report is completed, reporting errors to the team member must be done. Once reporting an error to the relevant member of the team should be executed: stamping all documents, checking the date to avoid penalties. The process is now completed.  ## Artifacts  - `account_payable_process_145.gv` - `account_payable_process_145.pdf`
