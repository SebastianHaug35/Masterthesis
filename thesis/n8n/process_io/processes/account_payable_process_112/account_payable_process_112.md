# account payable process 112  ## Source Description  The text below is about the payment process. It starts with entering the order details. Once the purchase order details are entered, a receiving report must be created. The receiving report should be collected after the reporting is created. When the collecting is completed, the process is divided into 2 parallel branches : checking date to avoid penalty charges and entering invoices into the account payable. Once the check of dates is to avoid penalties, the creation of a voucher for documents is also necessary. Now the process is completed.  ## Artifacts  - `account_payable_process_112.gv` - `account_payable_process_112.pdf`
