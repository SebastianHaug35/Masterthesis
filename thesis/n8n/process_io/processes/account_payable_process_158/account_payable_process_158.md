# account payable process 158  ## Source Description  The text below is about the account payable process, which begins when you enter your purchase order details. Once the purchase order details are entered into your system, you need to create a receipt report. After creating a receiving report the vendors invoice needs to be collected. Once the vendor invoice has been collected, the process is split into 3 parallel branches : the payment schedule, stamping all documents and entry of the invoice into accounts payable account. After the payment is scheduled, the next tasks should be checking dates to avoid penalty charges and storing in paid invoice file, they should be done in parallel. Once a date check to avoid penalty charges occurs, creating a voucher should be done for the documents. The process has now been completed.  ## Artifacts  - `account_payable_process_158.gv` - `account_payable_process_158.pdf`
