# account payable process 198  ## Source Description  The following description is about the Accounts Receivable process and begins when you mail your senior accountant to your department. After sending to the senior accountant in your department, creating a receiving report needs to be done. After creating a receipt report, you must confirm all documents have been amended. After confirmation that all documents have been modified, one or more of the following paths should be executed : stamping all documents, creating a voucher for the documents. After stamping all documents, the process is split into two parallel branches: entering the invoice into the Accounts payable and checking dates to avoid penalty charges. Once creating the voucher for the documents occurs, one or more of the following paths should be executed: scheduling the payment, and storing it in the bill file. The process is now completed.  ## Artifacts  - `account_payable_process_198.gv` - `account_payable_process_198.pdf`
